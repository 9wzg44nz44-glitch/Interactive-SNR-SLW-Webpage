The Landscape of Scalar & Longitudinal Waves

Standard Maxwell equations describe transverse electromagnetic waves (TEM) where E and B are perpendicular to the direction of propagation. However, extensions using quaternions and biquaternions (as developed in Haralick's framework and earlier by others) allow for longitudinal electric fields (E parallel to propagation) and a scalar field S = ∇·A + (1/c²)∂φ/∂t when the Lorenz gauge condition is relaxed or when specific hardware excites curl-free potentials.

Two prominent experimental lines of inquiry stand out in recent decades:

ZIMMERMAN 2011
QEX + Journal of Applied Physics

Longitudinal Vector Potential Az via Plasma AB Detector

  • → Tx: Monopole antenna in TM01 circular waveguide or open aperture with screen (nulls on-axis TEM)
  • → Rx: Folded plasma tube (mercury vapor, 200 mA DC bias) acting as differential Aharonov-Bohm detector
  • → Key result: Az propagates as 1/R; Ez as 1/R² (near-field dominant on axis). |A/E| ≈ 0.3. Plasma electrons respond where metal valence electrons cannot.
  • → Theory: Macroscopic classical AB effect; momentum transfer p = eA; validates Lorenz gauge for propagating A waves.
HIVELY 2016
US Patent 9,306,527 B1

Scalar-Longitudinal Waves (SLW) via Bifilar Pancake Coils

  • → Tx/Rx: Bifilar pancake (flat spiral) coil with alternating winding directions so adjacent turns carry current in opposite directions → strong B-field cancellation
  • → Drive: Short rise-time repetitive disruptive negative unipolar pulses (Tesla/Boyce inspired)
  • → Key mechanism: Gradient-driven current; curl-free vector potential emphasized; generation of longitudinal E and scalar magnetic S field
  • → Theory: Fits Haralick biquaternion framework where S ≠ 0 yields electro-scalar waves Ez = f(z−ct), S = f(z−ct)/c. Practical hardware for SLW comms, imaging, etc.

Key Scalar Field
S = ∇ · A + (1/c²) ∂φ/∂t
When S ≠ 0 (deviation from strict Lorenz gauge), longitudinal solutions appear.

From the slides (Haralick):

"When Ex = Ey = 0 and S ≠ 0, the general solution for the forward wave is Ez(z,t) = f(z − ct) and S(z,t) = f(z − ct)/c. These are the Electro-Scalar wave fields."

ZIMMERMAN EMPHASIS
Longitudinal A-field propagation
Experimental demonstration that Az carries momentum and propagates ~1/R even when TEM is nulled on-axis. Plasma detector essential because metal electrons are too slow (v ~ mm/s vs plasma 40 km/s DC + RF kick ~20 m/s).
HIVELY EMPHASIS
Bifilar B-cancellation + pulsed gradient drive
Hardware designed so magnetic field from adjacent turns cancels. Short rise-time negative pulses create charge densification/rarefaction pairs that constitute the longitudinal electric wave. Aligns with Tesla concatenated resonance ideas.
SHARED FOUNDATION
Potentials are physically real
Both validate that electromagnetic potentials (A, φ, S) are observable and can carry information independently of or in addition to the transverse E/B fields. Consistent with correspondence principle (classical limit of AB effect).

Zimmerman vs Hively: Key Dimensions

Dimension Zimmerman (2011) Hively (US9306527, 2016)
Primary Field Detected/Transmitted Longitudinal vector potential Az (momentum-carrying) Scalar-longitudinal waves (SLW): longitudinal E + scalar S magnetic field
Transmitter Hardware Quarter-wave monopole in TM01 waveguide or screened open-ended waveguide (nulls TEM on axis) Bifilar pancake (flat spiral) coil with opposing adjacent turns + short rise-time negative pulses; also monopole examples at 8 GHz
Receiver Hardware Plasma tube (folded CFL, 200 mA DC) as differential AB detector. Bias reversal separates E and A contributions. Bifilar pancake coil (same as Tx) or monopole; sensitive to gradient-driven currents and SLW
Propagation Scaling (far field) Az ∝ 1/R (radiation term survives); Ez ∝ 1/R² (near-field, TEM nulled on-axis) Longitudinal E and scalar S supported by biquaternion solutions (Ez = f(z−ct)). Practical range demonstrated in patent context; less quantitative far-field data published.
Theoretical Anchor Classical macroscopic Aharonov-Bohm effect; interaction energy U = e(φ − v·A); Lorenz gauge required for propagating A waves Biquaternion / quaternion generalized Maxwell (Haralick lineage); S ≠ 0 enables electro-scalar waves; curl-free A emphasized via B-cancellation
Experimental Validation Waveguide (3 m) + free-space to 25 m; VNA phase/amplitude separation of E/A; plasma responsivity ~420 kA/(Wb/m) Patent-focused; hardware prototypes implied (bifilar coils, pulse generators). Less peer-reviewed quantitative propagation data than Zimmerman.
Comms Strengths Rigorous proof of A-wave existence & detection; potential for covert comms (no TEM power on axis); works at L-band microwave Tesla-inspired pulsed operation; hardware simplicity (no plasma needed); B-cancellation may reduce some losses; broader "scalar" claims (imaging, fusion?)
Limitations / Open Questions Plasma detector requires high DC bias & careful implementation; on-axis E weak at long range Fewer independent replications published; quantitative SNR/range data less detailed in public literature
Note: Both approaches are compatible with the biquaternion framework presented by Haralick. Zimmerman provides the strongest experimental evidence to date for propagating longitudinal A at microwave frequencies. Hively provides practical, Tesla-derived hardware optimized for scalar-longitudinal generation and reception.

Key Takeaways & Synthesis

Zimmerman's Strength

Provides the most rigorous, peer-reviewed experimental confirmation of propagating longitudinal vector potential waves at microwave frequencies, including quantitative scaling laws and a working plasma-based detector. Excellent foundation for understanding the physics.

Hively's Strength

Delivers practical, patent-protected hardware (bifilar pancake + pulsed drive) that is easier to implement than plasma tubes for many applications. Strong alignment with Tesla's vision and the biquaternion scalar S field.

Complementary Future

A hybrid system — Zimmerman-style rigorous A-wave physics + Hively bifilar/pulsed generation — could yield robust scalar-longitudinal communication links, especially for covert, through-obstacle, or space applications where TEM is undesirable or blocked.
